Our Services

Comprehensive Water Quality Testing Solutions

Drinking Water Analysis

We test water from wells, springs, pipelines, and bottled water facilities to ensure compliance with Philippine National Standards for Drinking Water (PNSDW). Parameters include:

  • Physical: Color, Odor, Taste, Turbidity, Temperature
  • Chemical: pH, Total Dissolved Solids, Hardness, Heavy Metals (Lead, Arsenic, Cadmium, etc.), Nitrates, Chlorides
  • Microbiological: Total Coliform, E. coli, Fecal Coliform, Heterotrophic Plate Count

Wastewater & Effluent Testing

Essential for industries, hotels, hospitals, and commercial establishments to comply with DENR-EMB standards for discharge. We analyze:

  • Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD)
  • Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD)
  • Total Suspended Solids (TSS)
  • Oil & Grease
  • Surfactants, Phosphates, and other pollutants

Recreational Water Testing

For swimming pools, resorts, beaches, and water parks to ensure safety for public use:

  • Free & Total Chlorine
  • pH Level
  • Alkalinity & Hardness
  • Microbial contamination

Sampling & Consultancy Services

We offer professional on-site sampling by our trained technicians, plus expert consultation on water treatment, compliance, and quality improvement.
