About Us

Committed to Water Safety and Quality Assurance

Who We Are

Aguarantee Laboratory Water Testing Services is a premier water quality testing facility based in Midsayap, Cotabato, Philippines. Established with a mission to protect public health and the environment, we provide accurate, reliable, and timely analysis of all types of water — from drinking water and groundwater to wastewater, industrial process water, and recreational water.

We are fully licensed and accredited by the Department of Health (DOH) and currently appying for the Environmental Management Bureau (EMB) accreditation. Our laboratory operates under strict quality management systems to ensure every result we deliver is precise, valid, and legally acceptable for compliance purposes.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality, affordable, and accessible water testing services that help individuals, businesses, and government agencies ensure water is safe, compliant, and fit for its intended use.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and respected water testing laboratory in Mindanao, recognized for excellence, integrity, and contribution to a healthier, safer community.
